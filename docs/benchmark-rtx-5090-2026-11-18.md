## Observations & Methodological Notes

- **`openfal` optimization progression (2026-11-18 baseline → 2026-11-19)**:
  - The initial `2026-11-18` `openfal` sweep ([070636.json](../benchmark/results/bench_NVIDIA_GeForce_RTX_5090_20261118_070636.json), [072017.json](../benchmark/results/bench_NVIDIA_GeForce_RTX_5090_20261118_072017.json)) ran conservative block offloading (`12.0–17.1 GB` peak VRAM) with BF16 dequantized `F.linear` and PyTorch's Windows-default `EFFICIENT_ATTENTION` (`7.00s` `qwen21_t2i`, `12.30s` `minimax_h3_t2i`, `182.74s` `wan22_i2v_14b`, `90.30s` `minimax_h3_i2v`).
  - Enabling dynamic VRAM residency (`compute_resident_blocks`), native CUDA Tensor Core GEMMs (`torch._scaled_mm` FP8 and `torch._int_mm` INT8), cuDNN Flash Attention SDPA priority (`prefer_cudnn_sdpa`), pinned host-RAM fast H2D / O(1) zero-D2H swapping (`PinnedModuleStager`), and warm residency/conditioning caches delivered **1.67x–3.54x warm speedups across all four workloads** (`3.98s`, `3.47s`, `109.14s`, `52.78s`) in pure PyTorch on Windows while still keeping `4.5–12.0 GB` lower peak VRAM than `ComfyUI` on `MiniMax-H3` and `Wan 2.2`. All four optimized outputs were visually inspected (plus `ffprobe` / `volumedetect` verification on the MP4 video+audio streams).
- **Engine tradeoffs on 32 GiB VRAM**:
  - `openfal` leads `minimax_h3_t2i` (`3.47s`, `1.10x` faster than `ComfyUI` and `18.72x` faster than `vLLM-Omni`), beats `ComfyUI` on `qwen21_t2i` (`3.98s` vs `5.32s`), stays within `9%` of `ComfyUI` on `minimax_h3_i2v` (`52.78s` vs `48.34s`, `2.21x` faster than `vLLM-Omni`), and starts its HTTP API in `1.8s` vs `18.6s` (`ComfyUI`) and `53–129s` (`vLLM-Omni`).
  - `vLLM-Omni` leads both Alibaba workloads (`qwen21_t2i` at `2.45s`, `wan22_i2v_14b` at `75.58s`) under Linux WSL2 using `--quantization fp8` (and `--enable-cpu-offload` for Wan 2.2 MoE). On MiniMax-H3, `vLLM-Omni` loads dequantized `BF16` weights (`40.23 GB` DiT + `51.51 GB` `Qwen3VL-32B`) and streams layers via `DistributedLayerwiseOffloadBackend` (`checkpoint_mmap`, `--dlo-resident-layers 12`, `--enforce-eager`, `CUDNN_ATTN`), which keeps VRAM to `14.2–16.3 GB` but makes PCIe host-to-device streaming the dominant bottleneck.
  - `ComfyUI` leads `minimax_h3_i2v` (`48.34s`) and places second on `minimax_h3_t2i` (`3.83s`) and `wan22_i2v_14b` (`85.87s`), using `~31 GB` of the 32 GiB VRAM budget on the larger models.
- **`minimax_h3_i2v` frame count constraint**: `openfal` and `ComfyUI` generate `73` frames (`3.04s` at `24 fps`). Because `vLLM-Omni` rejects video durations under `4s`, [bench_runner.py](../benchmark/bench_runner.py) passes `num_frames=97` for `minimax_h3_i2v`, which `vLLM-Omni` decodes as `107` frames (`4.46s` at `24 fps`) plus `32 kHz` stereo AAC audio.
- **`minimax_h3_t2i` native resolution**: Passing `extra_params={"min_generation_area": 1024}` ensures `vLLM-Omni` renders directly at `1344x768` instead of upscaling to its default `2048²` generation canvas (`2720x1536`).
